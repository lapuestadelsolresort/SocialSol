## Why this is a good example

Hook angle is direct-intro: no portfolio reference, no question, just who-we-are + why-them + link. Used when enrichment is thin. Why-contacting disclosure is explicit (`Your name came up in a list of Texas-based planners doing destination work`). Body absorbs the elevator-sentence nouns. Compact at 3 sentences plus closer — within the constraint window's lower band. One proof point (late-2026 + early-2027 bookings). Closer pivots in the `easy to reach` register. Two em-dashes total. Zero warmth markers in the body; sign-off `All my very best,` carries the warmth. **Composer note:** direct-intro is the right pick when enrichment yields no usable hook — better to be honest and short than to fabricate a connection.
